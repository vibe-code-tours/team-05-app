# CrossMart Live Demo Walkthrough

This document serves as a step-by-step guide for presenting a live demonstration of the CrossMart web application.

## 1. Homepage & First Impressions
*Goal: Show off the premium UI and initial value proposition.*

1. **Navigate to the Homepage:** Open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. **Hero Banner:** Point out the primary Call to Action and the premium, dynamic design aesthetics that set us apart from generic marketplaces.
3. **Featured Products & Categories:** Scroll down to the Category Grid and Featured Products. Mention how buyers can easily discover items without endlessly scrolling a Facebook feed.
4. **Promo Banners:** Highlight the "Free Shipping" and "Refer a Friend" promotional banners, showing how the platform incentivizes buyers.

## 2. Exploring Products
*Goal: Highlight the unique product types (In-Stock vs Cargo).*

1. **Click on "Products":** Navigate to the `/products` page.
2. **Filters & Categories:** Demonstrate the sidebar filters.
3. **Select a Cargo Product:** Specifically choose a product tagged as a "Cargo Item" (e.g., coming from Bangkok).
4. **Product Details:** Emphasize the distinct information shown for Cargo items, such as the estimated delivery timeline and cross-border shipping fees.

## 3. The Core USP: Cargo Tracking
*Goal: Demonstrate the transparent tracking pipeline.*

1. **Navigate to Cargo Tracking:** Click on the "Cargo Tracking" link in the header or user dashboard.
2. **Enter a Tracking ID:** (Use a sample tracking ID from the seeded database, e.g., `TRK-123456`).
3. **Explain the Milestones:** Walk the audience through the visual pipeline:
   - *Order Placed*
   - *Purchased in Bangkok*
   - *In Transit to Yangon*
   - *Arrived at Hub*
   - *Out for Delivery*
4. **Value Add:** Remind the audience that this replaces dozens of manual chat messages between the buyer and seller.

## 4. Shopping Cart & Checkout
*Goal: Show the frictionless checkout flow.*

1. **Add to Cart:** Add the product to the shopping cart.
2. **View Cart:** Open the cart slide-out or page.
3. **Checkout Process:** Proceed to checkout. Mention that local payment methods (KPay, WavePay) are supported, and users can upload payment proof for verification.

## 5. (Optional) Seller / Admin Dashboard
*Goal: Show how we solve seller burnout.*

1. **Login as Seller:** Log in using a verified seller account.
2. **Order Management:** Show the dashboard where a seller can view all active orders in one place.
3. **Updating Tracking:** Show how a seller can click one button to update an order's cargo status, which instantly updates the buyer's tracking page.

## Conclusion of Demo
*Script: "As you can see, CrossMart completely transforms the cross-border buying experience from a fragmented, stressful chat into a seamless, transparent e-commerce journey. Thank you!"*
